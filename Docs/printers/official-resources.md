Light Magenta `9D7P9A` · Overcoat `9D7Q0A` · Optimizer `9D7Q1A` · **White `9D7Q3A`**.
Printheads (HP 886, R-series family): White `G0Z21A` · Colour/Overcoat `G0Z24A`.
- [HP 875 3L Black — confirms "for HP Latex R530"](https://www.wensco.com/itemdetail/9D7P7A) — `[verified]`

> ⚠ The R530 uses the **HP 875** ink family — *not* HP 872 (R1000/R2000) or HP 832/873 (other models).
> The 886 printhead numbers are the shared R-series part; confirm against your R530's front-panel
> supply screen on install.
